01 • Branch Identity & Technical Focus

About the CMU IEEE Student Branch

Based at Caribbean Maritime University on the Palisadoes Peninsula in Kingston, Jamaica, our IEEE Student Branch connects aspiring engineers across mechatronics, marine electrical power, industrial automation, and computer science to solve real-world Caribbean challenges.

Mechatronics & Robotics

Hands-on microcontroller labs, PCB prototyping, sensor fusion, and closed-loop electromechanical design.

Smart IoT Telemetry

Building low-power wireless sensor nodes and cloud dashboards for water catchment and environmental resilience.

Marine & Hybrid Power

Advancing AC/DC hybrid emergency hardware and maritime electrical systems.

Edge AI & Vision

Developing Python and browser-based computer vision applications for interactive demos and precision agriculture.
